CPU vs GPU parity

Runs the CPU kernel and the WebGL kernel on the same inputs and compares the pixels. Each result is composited over black and over white (the two worst-case backdrops) before comparing, so differences in colour and in alpha both show. A scenario passes when no channel differs by more than 8 levels and fewer than 1% differ by more than 2. Contrast guarantees are also measured on the GPU output.
